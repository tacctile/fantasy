## Build

**Item 1 — `parsePlayoffRules` / `getPlayoffRules` (`src/services/playoff-picture.ts`)** → `[x]`

Reads `settings.playoff_teams`, `playoff_type`, `playoff_round_type`, `divisions` from `league_config.roster_settings_raw` under the ADR escape hatch, and reuses the shipped `parsePlayoffWeekStart` from `dashboard.ts` rather than growing a second boundary parser. Every field nullable; `asPositiveInteger` rejects strings, floats, zero, negatives, and a field size of 1. `firstRoundByes` inferred arithmetically and typed as such. `seedingBasis: 'recomputed_record'` is in the type so a reader never has to guess the basis. **This item is what finally closes the playoff-fields gap** carried in `league-context.ts`'s KNOWN GAP block and in STATE.yml.

**Item 2 — `computePlayoffPicture` / `getPlayoffPicture`** → `[x]`

Deterministic bounding, never simulation. Floor = current win-equivalent (a team can lose out); ceiling = floor + games remaining. CLINCHED when fewer than N others have a ceiling reaching its floor; ELIMINATED when at least N others have a floor strictly above its ceiling; otherwise CONTROLS OWN PATH if winning out alone suffices, else NEEDS HELP.

**The defect this fold caught, and the reason item 3 exists as its own item:** the first draft used `>=` in BOTH the clinch and the elimination predicates. Walking a concrete scenario for the test suite — four teams, a two-team field, two teams level at 1-1 — showed that this prints **"eliminated" on a team merely tied for the last spot**, which is not a conservative reading, it is a false statement. The fix is a deliberate asymmetry: a tie counts AGAINST the team when clinching (a level rival could pass it on a tiebreaker) and FOR it when eliminating (it could win that same tiebreaker). Both verdicts are then unfalsifiable however points-for moves. This is now the module's most heavily documented invariant and has its own named test.

Second correction from the same pass: a team with no games played AND none remaining now returns `undetermined` rather than being handed a verdict. Without it, a preseason/unsynced league rendered every team as "needs help" — a claim made from zero evidence.

`resolveMagicNumber` is gated on `resolveStatus`'s own verdict rather than re-deriving the predicates, so the badge and the number cannot drift apart. The number is SEARCHED upward over games actually remaining; a closed-form formula would encode an assumed league shape, which is the thing this wave exists not to do.

The remaining schedule is matchup rows with a `native_matchup_id` and no points yet — this is the one Wave 5 service that keeps unscored rows, and a half-scored pair counts as remaining for the unscored side only. Seeding is win PERCENTAGE desc (not raw wins — an unsynced week leaves teams on different games played, and raw wins would rank a team that has simply played more above one that has lost less) → points-for → roster id. Byes and anomalous group sizes are no-game, identical posture to `luck.ts`, so the two surfaces can never disagree about who played whom.
