**Declared silence (at decision time, not post-hoc):** no wiki page defines (a) a per-week LEAGUE-AVERAGE line for a cumulative team-score chart, (b) how to treat a bye/missing week inside a cumulative series, or (c) team-level (as opposed to player-level) variance framing. `ROUTING.md`'s nearest rows are "Points for/against luck analysis" and "Consistency score / boom-bust rate" — both read, neither covers these. Decisions taken and disclosed: the league line is the plain mean of the teams PRESENT at that week (a bye is absent, never a zero — folding a bye in as 0 would drag the reference line down and libel the whole league); a missing week CARRIES the previous cumulative total forward rather than dropping to zero, and the line simply does not start until a team's first scored week; the player-level consistency framing is applied to team-weeks by analogy, which the page's own reasoning (level-preserving floor/ceiling over unnormalized dispersion) transfers to cleanly. ## What shipped, item by item

**Item 1 — `services/score-trends.ts`.** `getScoreTrends` (matchups + `league_config.roster_settings_raw` + rosters, explicit columns) over a pure `computeScoreTrends`. Per team: week-aligned series, cumulative, total/average, low/median/high, and a `seriesIndex` assigned by `native_roster_id` ascending (a STABLE key — re-sorting the page can never repaint a team, the rule `series.ts` states, enforced at the data layer instead of per chart). League: per-week mean, cumulative mean, median team-week, counted/non-final weeks, `lowConfidence`. Two private helpers in `dashboard.ts` (`fetchRosterNames`, `parsePlayoffWeekStart`) were EXPORTED rather than duplicated — additive, no behaviour change, one parser for the regular-season boundary across the admin surface.

**Items 2 + 4 — `weekly-points-section.tsx` (+ `weekly-points-bars.tsx`).** Bars, never a line. The ≤4-series cap is enforced INSIDE the section (grouped bars under it, `SmallMultiples` over it) rather than left to the caller — a 10-team league is the normal case here, and the decision shouldn't be re-made per call site. Bars measure from the ZERO line, not the plot floor, so a negative week renders below the baseline; a bye renders NO bar (a zero-height bar sitting on the axis is a different claim — "they scored nothing").

**Item 3 — `cumulative-trend-chart.tsx`.** The one correct line chart, always carrying the dashed neutral league-average reference. Past the cap it fans out to small multiples with the league line REPEATED inside every facet on the shared domain, so a facet answers "ahead or behind" alone. Missing weeks break the polyline into segments rather than interpolating across a week never played.

**Item 5 — `score-spread-chart.tsx`.** Floor→ceiling band with a median notch per team, ranked by median, on one shared axis with the league median as a dotted reference. Not zero-anchored (a range band, not a magnitude bar). Deliberately min/max rather than p10/p90: ~14 observations can't support percentile interpolation. Carries the under-6-weeks caveat; the raw weekly bars deliberately do NOT (they're observations, not inferences).

**Item 6 — `score-sparkline.tsx` + `team-trend-list.tsx`.** Row-sized trend with no axis/title/tooltip, deliberately NOT inside `ChartFrame` (a figure/figcaption in a list row is structurally wrong before it looks wrong). All rows share one domain — a per-row rescale would make every team's trend identical. Drill-down is `?team=<native_roster_id>`, the Wave 4 URL-state pattern, so the expanded view is a shareable link and the section stays server-rendered.

**Item 7 — the route.** `app/(admin)/leagues/[leagueId]/score-trends/{page,loading}.tsx` + `score-trends-shell.tsx`; sidebar row moved from FUTURE_NAV to PRIMARY_NAV. Identity resolves through `getLeagueContext` (Wave 5's mandated resolver) → unknown league 404s and the header survives; the trends query runs through `settleQuery` → a fault renders `SectionUnavailable` inside the page rather than reaching the route error boundary. No new `error.tsx`: the `[leagueId]` segment boundary already covers this child. Drill-down for an unknown roster id falls back to the league view rather than 404ing (a stale link to a departed team should still show the section).

## Judgment calls disclosed

- `fetchRosterNames`/`parsePlayoffWeekStart` exported from `dashboard.ts` instead of copied — chose one-definition drift-safety over module purity.
- Page resolves context via `getLeagueContext` AND settles the trends query separately: two reads of `leagues`/`league_config` per render, bought deliberately so a failed section keeps a titled header (the Wave 4 posture) with only one data section on the page.
- `teamLabel` is a local convention twin of the draft board's `rosterLabel`, not an import — a chart section reaching into draft-board UI to name a team would couple two surfaces with no other relationship.
- Sparkline bypasses `ChartFrame` (see item 6 above).
- Spread chart keeps the whole league visible while drilled in; the other two charts narrow. A lone team's spread with nothing to compare against answers nothing, which is the reading the item asks the chart to contextualise.
